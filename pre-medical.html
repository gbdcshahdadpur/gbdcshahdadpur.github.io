H.Sc. Pre-Medical

HSC-I & HSC-II

A science-based intermediate program designed to provide students with strong foundations in Biology, Chemistry and Physics for higher education in medical and life-science fields.

Home Academics Pre-Medical
Intermediate Program

About H.Sc. Pre-Medical

H.Sc. Pre-Medical is a two-year intermediate program offered at Government Boys Degree College Shahdadpur. It prepares students for higher education in medicine, dentistry, pharmacy, nursing and other biological sciences.

Students study Biology, Chemistry and Physics along with compulsory subjects. The program combines classroom learning with laboratory practical work and develops scientific understanding, observation and analytical skills.

Biology

Study of life and living organisms

Chemistry

Matter, reactions and chemical processes

Physics

Physical laws and scientific principles

Practical Work

Laboratory-based scientific learning

Course Structure

Subjects in H.Sc. Pre-Medical

Students study science subjects along with compulsory subjects during HSC-I and HSC-II.

HSC-I

First Year / Class XI

Biology

Study of cells, biological molecules, tissues, organisms, biodiversity and basic life processes.

Chemistry

Fundamental concepts of chemistry, atomic structure, chemical bonding, states of matter and chemical reactions.

Physics

Measurements, vectors, motion, forces, work, energy, circular motion and basic physical principles.

English

Grammar, comprehension, vocabulary, communication and English literature.

Urdu / Sindhi

Language, literature, comprehension, writing and communication skills according to the applicable board scheme.

Islamiat / Ethics

Islamic studies for Muslim students and the applicable alternative subject for non-Muslim students.

HSC-II

Second Year / Class XII

Biology

Human physiology, reproduction, genetics, evolution, ecology and other advanced biological concepts.

Chemistry

Organic chemistry, chemical equilibrium, electrochemistry and important chemical processes.

Physics

Electricity, magnetism, electromagnetic phenomena, modern physics and related scientific concepts.

English

Advanced language skills, comprehension, writing, grammar and literature.

Urdu / Sindhi

Language and literature studies according to the applicable examination board scheme.

Pakistan Studies

Pakistan's history, geography, constitution, society and national development.

Note: Subject names and compulsory-subject combinations may vary according to the current examination scheme issued by the relevant Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education. Students should follow the latest official scheme of studies.

Laboratory Learning

Practical Work

Practical classes help students connect theoretical concepts with real scientific observations.

Biology Practical

Microscopy, biological specimens, tissues, experiments and observation of biological structures.

Chemistry Practical

Laboratory experiments, chemical reactions, identification tests and safe laboratory practices.

Physics Practical

Measurements, experiments, instruments, physical laws and verification of scientific principles.

Admission Information

Eligibility & Preparation

Eligibility

  • Students who have successfully completed Matriculation / SSC or equivalent qualification.
  • Students should meet the admission requirements prescribed by the relevant education authorities.
  • Preference may be given according to merit where applicable.

Recommended Skills

  • Strong interest in Biology and natural sciences.
  • Regular study and laboratory practice.
  • Good analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Effective English reading and writing skills.
Future Opportunities

Career & University Pathways

After H.Sc. Pre-Medical, students can pursue a variety of health, medical and life-science related fields.

MBBS

Medical education leading to a career as a doctor.

BDS

Dental education and professional dentistry.

Pharmacy

Pharmaceutical sciences and healthcare.

Nursing

Professional nursing and healthcare services.

Biotechnology

Biological research, technology and laboratory sciences.

Microbiology

Study of microorganisms and laboratory sciences.

Biological Sciences

Higher studies in biology and related disciplines.

Allied Health Sciences

Various healthcare and diagnostic professions.
