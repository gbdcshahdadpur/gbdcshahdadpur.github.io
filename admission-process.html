Admission Process

Students seeking admission to Government Boys Degree College Shahdadpur should follow the admission instructions announced by the college and relevant authorities.

Step 1 — Check Eligibility

Review the eligibility requirements for the desired program.

Step 2 — Complete Application

Submit the required admission application and supporting documents.

Step 3 — Verification

Submitted documents will be checked according to the applicable admission requirements.

Step 4 — Admission Confirmation

Successful applicants should complete the remaining admission formalities within the announced deadline.
