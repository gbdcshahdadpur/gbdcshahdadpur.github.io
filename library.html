Library & Digital Library

A supportive academic learning environment providing students with access to books, digital resources, computers, multimedia facilities and modern learning technology.

Our Library

Government Boys Degree College Shahdadpur provides students with a well-established library facility designed to support classroom learning, self-study, research and academic development. The library contains approximately more than 8,000 books covering a wide range of academic subjects and general educational interests. Students can use the library as a quiet and supportive place for reading, preparation and independent study.

Digital Learning Environment

Digital Library

The college has a dedicated digital library facility where students can use computers for digital learning, academic research, online educational resources and other technology-based learning activities.

The digital library is equipped with 12 computers and provides students with a convenient environment for accessing digital educational material. Multimedia facilities further enhance the learning experience.

  • 12 computers available for student use
  • Digital learning and academic research
  • Multimedia-supported learning
  • Student access to digital educational resources
  • Computer-based academic activities
DIGITAL & MULTIMEDIA FACILITIES

Technology for Modern Learning

Modern technology facilities help students and teachers use multimedia and digital resources to make academic learning more effective.

Computer Facilities

Twelve computers are available in the digital library for students to use for academic learning, research and digital educational activities.

Multimedia Screen

A multimedia screen is available to support presentations, demonstrations and technology-assisted teaching and learning.

Television

A television facility is available for educational viewing and multimedia-based academic activities.

Speaker System

A speaker system supports multimedia presentations, educational content and other academic activities.

Established Computer Lab

The college has an established computer laboratory that supports practical computer education and digital learning.

Digital Learning

Students can make use of digital facilities to complement classroom teaching and broaden their access to educational resources.

A Facility for Student Learning

The library and digital library together provide a supportive academic environment where students can read books, conduct academic research, use computers, access digital educational resources and benefit from multimedia facilities. These facilities are intended to encourage independent learning, improve digital literacy and support students in their academic journey.
