ABOUT OUR COLLEGE

Government Boys Degree College Shahdadpur

Government Boys Degree College Shahdadpur is a public-sector educational institution serving students from Shahdadpur and surrounding areas of District Sanghar, Sindh.

The college is committed to providing quality education, developing academic abilities, promoting discipline and character building, and preparing students for higher education and professional life.

As an institution of the College Education Department, Government of Sindh, the college continues to contribute to the educational and social development of the region.

OUR JOURNEY

History & Institutional Background

Government Boys Degree College Shahdadpur has a proud history of serving the educational needs of Shahdadpur and its surrounding communities.

Before 1978, the college was supported through the contributions and efforts of local philanthropists and the people of Shahdadpur. This reflected the community's strong commitment to education and the development of its youth.

In 1978, the college was taken over by the Government of Sindh under the nationalization policy. Since then, it has continued its educational mission as a public-sector institution under the College Education Department, Government of Sindh.

Over the years, the college has continued to provide educational opportunities to students and remains an important institution for higher education in the region.
