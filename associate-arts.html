Associate Degree in Arts (ADA / B.A)

2-Year Undergraduate Degree Program

A foundational 2-year degree in social sciences, humanities, and languages affiliated with the University of Sindh, fostering critical thinking and intellectual growth.

Undergraduate Level

About Associate Degree in Arts

The Associate Degree in Arts (ADA), which succeeds the former Bachelor of Arts (B.A) degree, is a two-year higher education program structured according to Higher Education Commission (HEC) guidelines and affiliated with the University of Sindh.

This program offers students a broad liberal arts education across political science, history, economics, literature, and sociology. It is specifically designed to cultivate analytical reasoning, written communication skills, and social research awareness, preparing graduates for competitive examinations, civil services, or seamless transition into 5th semester BS programs.

Political Science

Statecraft, constitutional developments, political theory, and international relations

Social Sciences

Sociological studies, economic principles, history, and civic structure analysis

Language & Literature

Advanced linguistic proficiency in English, Sindhi, Urdu, and analytical writing

BS Lateral Entry

Direct eligibility for 5th semester BS admission in Arts and Social Science disciplines

Course Structure

ADA Part-I & Part-II Subject Options

Students complete compulsory courses along with elective combinations approved by the University of Sindh.

ADA Part I

First Year (Part I) Curriculum

English Compulsory

Functional English, essay writing skills, comprehension, and foundational literature analysis.

Islamic Studies / Ethics

Islamic moral framework, trade ethics, and social responsibilities, or Ethics for non-Muslim students.

Political Science - I

Introduction to political theory, state institutions, sovereignty, rights, and government structures.

Economics - I

Microeconomic theory, consumer behavior, market structures, price determination, and supply-demand analysis.

Sociology - I

Fundamental sociological concepts, social structure, culture, socialization processes, and group dynamics.

History / Elective Literature

History of the Subcontinent / Islamic History, or elective studies in Sindhi and Urdu Literature.

ADA Part II

Second Year (Part II) Curriculum

Pakistan Studies

Historical movement, constitutional evolution, foreign relations, and contemporary socio-economic challenges.

English Literature / Composition

Advanced written expression, critical analysis of selected literary prose, and rhetoric.

Political Science - II

Constitutional history of Pakistan, comparative politics, and international political organizations.

Economics - II

Macroeconomic fundamentals, national income accounting, banking systems, public finance, and Pakistan's economy.

Sociology - II

Social institutions, rural-urban social issues in Pakistan, population dynamics, and development studies.

History - II / Elective Language

Modern world history, freedom movement studies, or advanced regional language and literature tracks.

Eligibility Criteria: Candidates holding Higher Secondary School Certificate (HSSC / Intermediate) in Arts, Science, Commerce, or equivalent qualification from a recognized Board are eligible for admission as per University of Sindh regulations.

Future Progression

Academic & Career Pathways

The ADA degree provides a versatile springboard for competitive examinations, educational careers, and advanced university degrees.

Civil Services (CSS / PCS)

Essential academic preparation for Federal (CSS) and Provincial (SPSC/PCS) public service commission examinations.

BS Program 5th Semester

Direct entry into 3rd year (5th semester) BS in English, Political Science, Sociology, Economics, or History.

Teaching & Education

Eligibility for B.Ed programs, school teacher recruitment examinations (PST, JEST), and educational roles.

Media & Journalism

Content writing, print/digital journalism, media reporting, public relations, and communications management.

Public Administration

Administrative assistant, office manager, and executive posts across government departments and NGOs.

Law (LL.B 3-Year / 5-Year)

Foundational qualification to pursue legal education (LAT examination and LL.B studies) at law faculties.
