Eligibility & Requirements

Admission eligibility criteria, guidelines, and document requirements for Intermediate and Associate Degree programs.

Students seeking admission to Government Boys Degree College Shahdadpur should review the eligibility requirements for their selected academic program and ensure that all required documents are available before applying.

Intermediate Programs

Students applying for Intermediate programs should have successfully completed the qualifying secondary-level examination required for admission.

  • Relevant secondary qualification
  • Required academic documents
  • Application according to the announced admission procedure
  • Program-specific subject requirements where applicable

Associate Degree Programs

Applicants seeking admission to Associate Degree programs should possess the relevant Intermediate-level qualification required for the selected program.

  • Relevant Intermediate qualification
  • Required academic documents
  • Program-specific subject background where applicable
  • Completion of the admission process

General Requirements

Applicants should carefully complete the admission application and provide accurate information.

  • Complete admission application
  • Valid academic record
  • Required supporting documents
  • Follow announced admission deadlines

Program Selection

Students should select a program according to their previous qualification, academic background and the subjects required for the selected program.

  • Review available programs
  • Check subject requirements
  • Select the appropriate program
  • Follow college admission instructions

Commonly Required Documents

Previous academic certificate
CNIC / B-Form as applicable
Marks sheet / marks certificate
Recent passport-size photographs
Other documents specified by the college
Additional documents where required
Important: Applicants should always follow the latest admission notice issued by the college or the relevant education authority. Program-specific eligibility, merit, deadlines and document requirements may vary.
