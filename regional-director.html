REGIONAL DIRECTOR COLLEGES

Prof. Lala Rukh Baloch

Regional Director Colleges, Shaheed Benazirabad Region
M.Sc (Computer Science) M.Ed M.S (I.T) College Education Department

"Education is the single most transformative tool for regional development. Our mandate is to cultivate modern academic environments across Shaheed Benazirabad Region where excellence, technology, and integrity guide student success."

Welcome to the official portal of Government Boys Degree College Shahdadpur.

As Regional Director of Colleges for the Shaheed Benazirabad Region, it is a privilege to extend my warm greetings to the students, faculty, staff, and visitors of this esteemed institution. The Regional Directorate of Colleges holds administrative oversight and educational governance over all public degree colleges—both boys and girls institutions—across three key districts of our region: District Shaheed Benazirabad (Nawabshah), District Sanghar, and District Naushahro Feroze.

Our primary mandate across these three districts is to ensure high standards of academic delivery, enforce institutional transparency, modernize learning infrastructure, and establish robust support systems for both students and faculty. With a professional background rooted in Information Technology, educational management, and strategic leadership, I am deeply committed to driving digital transformation, improving laboratory and library facilities, and empowering our teaching faculty through continuous professional development.

Government Boys Degree College Shahdadpur stands as a vital academic pillar within District Sanghar. The college plays a pivotal role in shaping the educational trajectory of the youth of Shahdadpur and its surrounding areas. By fostering a learning environment that combines rigorous academic standards with ethical values, character building, and technological readiness, we aim to prepare our young scholars for higher education and competitive professional careers.

Through close collaboration among regional academic leaders, dedicated college administration, our hardworking faculty, and local stakeholders, we continue our collective mission to elevate educational standards across all three districts. I encourage the students of GBDC Shahdadpur to make full use of the academic and extracurricular resources available at the college, remain dedicated to their studies, and strive for excellence in every endeavor.

Regional Educational Priorities

Digital & Tech Integration

Expanding IT resources, computer labs, and digital platforms across colleges in Shaheed Benazirabad, Sanghar, and Naushahro Feroze to keep students aligned with modern global technologies.

Academic Standards & Merit

Enhancing teaching methodology, maintaining exam integrity, and promoting continuous professional development for regional faculty across all public colleges.

Infrastructure Growth

Upgrading science laboratories, libraries, and classroom facilities to provide students throughout the three districts with optimal learning environments.
