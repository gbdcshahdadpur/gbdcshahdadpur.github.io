Intermediate Program

About F.Sc. Pre-Engineering

The Higher Secondary Certificate (HSC) Pre-Engineering program provides rigorous training in problem-solving, analytical thinking, and quantitative reasoning.

Designed under the Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education guidelines, it equips students with the exact competencies required to crack competitive university entry tests for engineering, computing, and technology fields.

Mathematics

Advanced quantitative reasoning and calculus

Physics

Physical mechanics, energy, and electronics

Chemistry

Chemical principles, reactions, and materials

Practical Labs

Hands-on experimental learning

Course Structure

Subjects in F.Sc. Pre-Engineering

Students study core science and mathematics subjects along with compulsory subjects during HSC-I and HSC-II.

HSC-I

First Year / Class XI

Mathematics-I

Algebra, trigonometry, functions, vectors, matrices, and analytical problem-solving.

Physics-I

Vectors, motion, work and energy, circular motion, fluid dynamics, and thermodynamics.

Chemistry-I

Atomic structure, chemical bonding, states of matter, stoichiometry, and reaction kinetics.

English

Grammar, comprehension, vocabulary, communication, and literature.

Urdu / Sindhi

Language, literature, comprehension, and writing skills according to the board scheme.

Islamiat / Ethics

Islamic studies for Muslim students and ethics for non-Muslim students.

HSC-II

Second Year / Class XII

Mathematics-II

Calculus, differential equations, analytic geometry, and advanced quantitative techniques.

Physics-II

Electrostatics, current electricity, electromagnetism, electronics, and modern physics.

Chemistry-II

Organic chemistry, environmental chemistry, industrial chemical processes, and equilibrium.

English

Advanced language skills, technical comprehension, essay writing, and literature.

Urdu / Sindhi

Language and literature studies according to the examination board scheme.

Pakistan Studies

History, geography, constitution, and national socio-economic development.

Note: Subject names and compulsory-subject combinations may vary according to the current examination scheme issued by the relevant Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education.

Future Pathways

Career & University Pathways

After F.Sc. Pre-Engineering, students can pursue engineering, technology, computer science, and analytical fields.

Core Engineering

Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics, Chemical, and Mechatronics Engineering.

Computing & IT

BS Computer Science, Software Engineering, AI, Data Science, and Cybersecurity.

Architecture & Design

Bachelor of Architecture (B.Arch), Urban Planning, and Structural Design.

Aeronautics & Avionics

Aerospace Engineering, Avionics Systems, and Renewable Energy Technology.

Applied Sciences

BS Mathematics, Actuarial Science, BS Physics, and Financial Analytics.

Entry Test Guidance

Preparation pathways for ECAT, MUET, NUST (NET), and FAST entry exams.
