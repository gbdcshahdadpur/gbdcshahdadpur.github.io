Associate Degree in Commerce (ADC / B.Com)

2-Year Undergraduate Degree Program

A career-oriented 2-year undergraduate program in accounting, business finance, taxation, and management practices affiliated with the University of Sindh.

Home Academics ADC / B.Com
Undergraduate Level

About Associate Degree in Commerce

The Associate Degree in Commerce (ADC), replacing the traditional Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com), is a comprehensive 2-year undergraduate program designed following Higher Education Commission (HEC) guidelines and affiliated with the University of Sindh.

This program delivers robust practical knowledge in financial accounting, auditing, taxation, business law, macroeconomics, and commercial practices. Graduates develop essential financial analytical skills required for direct industry entry or seamless transition into 5th semester BS Commerce / BBA programs.

Financial Accounting

Advanced ledger management, auditing standards, and statements

Business Law

Mercantile law, corporate compliance, and taxation frameworks

Economic Analysis

Macroeconomics, money, banking, and public finance principles

BS Lateral Entry

Direct eligibility for 5th semester BS Commerce or BS BBA degrees

Course Structure

ADC Part-I & Part-II Curriculum

The two-year curriculum covers core business administration, financial principles, and mandatory university requirements.

ADC Part I

First Year (Part I) Courses

Advanced Financial Accounting

Partnership accounts, corporate financial statements, branch accounting, and consignment procedures.

Business Statistics & Math

Data collection, measures of central tendency, probability, index numbers, interest, and annuities.

Money, Banking & Finance

Monetary economics, central banking systems, credit creation, commercial bank operation, and foreign exchange.

Principles of Management

Organizational planning, leadership styles, decision-making dynamics, human resource principles, and control mechanisms.

English Compulsory

Business communication skills, report writing, formal correspondence, and comprehension skills.

Islamic Studies / Ethics

Islamic ethical principles in trade and personal conduct, or Ethics for non-Muslim students.

ADC Part II

Second Year (Part II) Courses

Cost Accounting

Cost concepts, material control, labor accounting, factory overhead allocation, and job costing models.

Auditing & Taxation

Verification procedures, internal controls, audit reports, income tax ordinance, and sales tax regulations.

Mercantile & Business Law

Contract act, sale of goods act, negotiable instruments, partnership laws, and company law basics.

Economic Development of Pakistan

National economic planning, agricultural sector, industrial policies, trade balances, and fiscal planning.

Pakistan Studies

Ideological foundation, political movement, geographical resources, and modern socio-economic challenges.

Computer Applications in Business

Spreadsheet modelling for financial analysis, accounting software fundamentals, and database usage.

Eligibility Criteria: Candidates holding an Intermediate in Commerce (I.Com), Intermediate Science (Pre-Engineering/General Science), or equivalent qualification from a recognized Board are eligible for admission as per University of Sindh regulations.

Future Progression

Academic & Career Pathways

The ADC / B.Com qualification establishes a practical foundation for immediate placement in financial services or professional accounting certifications.

BS Commerce / BBA 5th Semester

Lateral entry into the 3rd year (5th semester) of BS Commerce, BS Accounting & Finance, or BBA programs.

Banking Sector

Bank officer positions, credit analysis, operations, cash management, and customer relationship roles.

Accountant & Bookkeeper

Maintenance of ledgers, financial statement preparation, payroll execution, and internal records handling.

Professional Certifications

Exemption path towards professional qualifications including CA, ACCA, CMA, and PIPFA.

Tax Consultancy & Audit

Assisting tax consultants, audit assistant roles in accounting firms, and corporate compliance filing.

Civil Services & Public Sector

Eligibility for provincial and federal competitive examinations requiring a graduate business degree.
