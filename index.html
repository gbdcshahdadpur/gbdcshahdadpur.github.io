WELCOME TO GBDC SHAHDADPUR

Empowering Students Through Quality Higher Education

Government Boys Degree College Shahdadpur is a premier public sector educational institution committed to academic excellence, discipline, and character building. Established under the College Education Department, Government of Sindh, we aim to deliver rigorous learning across science, commerce, and humanities.

Equipped with qualified faculty, modern computer laboratories, and rich library resources, GBDC Shahdadpur provides an enriching environment designed to unlock student potential and build a solid foundation for higher studies and successful careers.
