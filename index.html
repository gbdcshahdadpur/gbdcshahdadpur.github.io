WELCOME TO OUR COLLEGE

Quality Education for a Better Future

Government Boys Degree College Shahdadpur is a government educational institution committed to providing quality education to the students of Shahdadpur and surrounding areas.

The college offers Intermediate programs in Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering, Computer Science, Commerce and Humanities. It also offers Associate Degree Programs in Science, Commerce and Arts.

Our aim is to provide students with a quality academic environment, develop their skills and prepare them for higher education and professional life.
