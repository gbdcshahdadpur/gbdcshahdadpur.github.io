About Intermediate Education

Government Boys Degree College Shahdadpur offers a range of Intermediate programs designed to provide students with a strong academic foundation for university education and future professional careers. The college offers Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering, Computer Science, Commerce, and Arts/Humanities streams.

Pre-Medical

Science Program

The Pre-Medical program is designed for students interested in medicine, health sciences, biological sciences, and related fields. It develops a strong foundation in Biology, Chemistry, and Physics, together with language and compulsory subjects.

Subjects & Major Chapters

# Subject Main Chapters / Areas
1 Biology Cell Biology, Biological Molecules, Enzymes, Bioenergetics, Biodiversity, Kingdoms, Plant Biology, Human Biology, Genetics, Evolution, Reproduction, Ecology
2 Chemistry Atomic Structure, Chemical Bonding, States of Matter, Chemical Equilibrium, Acids & Bases, Thermochemistry, Organic Chemistry, Hydrocarbons, Functional Groups, Biochemistry
3 Physics Measurements, Vectors, Motion, Force, Work & Energy, Circular Motion, Gravitation, Waves, Thermodynamics, Electricity, Magnetism, Electromagnetism, Electronics, Modern Physics
4 English Grammar, Vocabulary, Comprehension, Writing Skills, Essay Writing, Communication Skills, Literature
5 Urdu / Sindhi Grammar, Prose, Poetry, Literature, Composition and Language Skills
6 Islamiyat / Ethics Quranic Studies, Seerah, Islamic Teachings, Islamic Values, Ethics and Moral Development
7 Pakistan Studies Ideology of Pakistan, History, Geography, Constitution, Culture, Economy and National Development

Pre-Engineering

Science Program

The Pre-Engineering program is designed for students who wish to pursue engineering, technology, physical sciences, and other technical disciplines. The program emphasizes Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry, developing analytical and problem-solving abilities.

Subjects & Major Chapters

# Subject Main Chapters / Areas
1 Mathematics Algebra, Matrices & Determinants, Functions, Quadratic Equations, Sequences & Series, Permutation & Combination, Binomial Theorem, Trigonometry, Analytical Geometry, Vectors, Limits, Differentiation, Integration
2 Physics Measurements, Vectors, Motion, Force, Work & Energy, Circular Motion, Gravitation, Properties of Matter, Waves, Thermodynamics, Electricity, Magnetism, Electromagnetism, Electronics, Modern Physics
3 Chemistry Atomic Structure, Chemical Bonding, States of Matter, Chemical Equilibrium, Acids & Bases, Thermochemistry, Electrochemistry, Organic Chemistry, Hydrocarbons, Functional Groups, Industrial Chemistry
4 English Grammar, Vocabulary, Comprehension, Composition, Essay Writing, Communication Skills, Literature
5 Urdu / Sindhi Grammar, Prose, Poetry, Literature, Composition and Language Skills
6 Islamiyat / Ethics Quranic Studies, Seerah, Islamic Teachings, Values, Ethics and Moral Development
7 Pakistan Studies Ideology of Pakistan, History, Geography, Constitution, Culture, Economy and National Development

Computer Science

Computing Program

The Computer Science program introduces students to computing, programming, information technology, and problem-solving. It provides a foundation for higher education in Computer Science, Information Technology, Software Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, and related fields.

Subjects & Major Chapters

# Subject Main Chapters / Areas
1 Computer Science Introduction to Computing, Computer Hardware, Software, Operating Systems, Data Representation, Number Systems, Programming Fundamentals, Algorithms, Flowcharts, Databases, Networks, Internet and Web Technologies
2 Mathematics Algebra, Functions, Matrices, Sequences & Series, Trigonometry, Coordinate Geometry, Vectors, Limits, Differentiation, Integration and Probability
3 Physics / Elective Measurements, Motion, Force, Work & Energy, Waves, Electricity, Magnetism, Electronics and Modern Physics
4 English Grammar, Vocabulary, Comprehension, Writing, Essay, Communication Skills and Literature
5 Urdu / Sindhi Grammar, Prose, Poetry, Literature, Composition and Language Skills
6 Islamiyat / Ethics Islamic Teachings, Quranic Studies, Seerah, Values, Ethics and Moral Development
7 Pakistan Studies History, Geography, Ideology, Constitution, Culture, Economy and National Development

Computer Science Focus

Programming fundamentals, algorithms and problem solving, computer hardware and software, databases, computer networks, internet technologies, web development and information technology.

Commerce

Business Program

The Commerce program provides students with fundamental knowledge of business, accounting, economics, finance, and commercial activities. It prepares students for further studies in Commerce, Business Administration, Accounting, Finance, Economics, Banking, and related disciplines.

Subjects & Major Chapters

# Subject Main Chapters / Areas
1 Principles of Accounting Introduction to Accounting, Accounting Concepts, Journal, Ledger, Trial Balance, Cash Book, Bank Reconciliation, Adjustments, Financial Statements, Depreciation
2 Principles of Commerce Introduction to Commerce, Trade, Business Organizations, Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, Joint Stock Companies, Banking, Insurance, Transportation, Warehousing
3 Economics Basic Economic Concepts, Demand & Supply, Production, Cost, Market Structures, National Income, Money & Banking, Inflation, Public Finance, International Trade
4 Business Mathematics / Statistics Percentages, Ratios, Proportion, Interest, Profit & Loss, Algebra, Statistics, Data Presentation, Measures of Central Tendency
5 English Grammar, Vocabulary, Comprehension, Writing Skills, Essay Writing, Communication and Literature
6 Urdu / Sindhi Grammar, Prose, Poetry, Literature, Composition and Language Skills
7 Islamiyat / Ethics & Pakistan Studies Islamic/Ethical Values, Pakistan Movement, History, Geography, Constitution, Culture and National Development

Commerce Focus

Accounting, business management, economics, banking, finance, entrepreneurship and commercial activities.
